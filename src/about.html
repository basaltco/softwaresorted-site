About SoftwareSorted

SoftwareSorted reviews the software UK tradespeople and small businesses actually compare when they're choosing a tool — job management apps for electricians, plumbers and heating engineers, appointment scheduling tools, and Making Tax Digital software for landlords.

Most software reviews online are written for a US audience, or by a vendor with a reason to steer you. We write for a UK reader making a real buying decision: prices in pounds where vendors publish them, and the things that matter here — CIS, VAT, Gas Safe, CP12, EICRs, Making Tax Digital and UK GDPR.

What you'll find here

  • Buyer's guides for each trade or business type, comparing the main tools side by side.
  • Single-tool reviews, each with a Quick Verdict, the real catches, and a "Who this is NOT for" section.
  • Head-to-head comparisons for the pairs of tools people most often weigh up against each other.
  • Compliance guides on how each tool handles CIS and VAT, Gas Safe and CP12 records, and data protection.

How the site is funded

Some links on this site are affiliate links: if you sign up through one, the vendor may pay us a commission, at no extra cost to you. The site may also carry advertising. Neither decides what we recommend. Tools without an affiliate programme are reviewed in the same way and to the same standard as tools with one, and several of our verdicts favour tools we currently earn nothing from.

What we won't do

  • Accept payment for a review, a better verdict or a higher placement.
  • Publish sponsored content.
  • Quote a user review we can't trace back to a named platform.
  • Present a feature as missing just because a vendor's marketing pages don't mention it — we check the help documentation first.

For the detail of how pages are researched and kept up to date, see How we review. To report a mistake or a price change, get in touch.
