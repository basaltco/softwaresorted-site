ServiceM8 vs Powered Now: Which Is Better for UK Electricians? (2026)

Quick Verdict

Once you see what each tool is really for, this one usually decides itself. ServiceM8 is an automation tool at its best on iPhone — excellent client communications, powerful custom forms, slick scheduling. Powered Now is a UK compliance tool — EICR certificates, CIS deductions, offline working, all built for British trades from day one.

For most UK electricians, compliance settles it. On devices: ServiceM8 says its Android app (ServiceM8 Lite) has almost all the same functionality as the iPhone app, but reviewers report it as weaker in practice, so if any engineer uses Android, test it on their phone during the free trial — or pick Powered Now, which works the same on both. If your team is on iPhone and compliance paperwork isn't a priority, ServiceM8's automation makes it genuinely compelling.

What These Tools Are Actually Built For

ServiceM8 and Powered Now serve similar audiences but come from fundamentally different directions. ServiceM8 is an Australian product built around making client-facing service delivery slicker — automated communications, fast job dispatch, clean payment collection. It's excellent at the customer experience layer of field service management.

Powered Now was built in the UK specifically to solve the paperwork problem for British tradespeople. Compliance certificates, CIS, MTD, working offline on sites with no signal — that's the product, not an add-on. For an electrician, that difference matters more than the feature list suggests.

The Android Gap in Detail

ServiceM8 says its Android app (ServiceM8 Lite) has "almost all the same functionality as the iOS app", including tracking time on site and clocking on and off. The gaps it lists are room scanning, drawing diagrams, editing PDFs and in-camera photo tagging. Reviewers still report the Android app lagging well behind the iPhone one. A UK Trustpilot reviewer who moved from Apple to Android wrote that "The android app has barely any functions that the apple one does to the point where its pretty pointless" (Chris Harvey, Mar 2026). If your team has Android users, test ServiceM8 on their phones during the free trial.

Powered Now has no such distinction. The Android and iOS apps are equivalent. If your team's devices are mixed, or if you hire engineers who prefer Android, Powered Now doesn't create a two-tier experience.

Where ServiceM8 Wins

Client communication automation. ServiceM8's automated messaging is the stand-out feature on its side of this comparison — "on my way" texts, arrival windows, job completion notifications, configurable follow-up sequences. For a domestic electrician where no-shows and late payments are a regular problem, this is the kind of automation that can pay for itself. Powered Now has standard client communications but doesn't match ServiceM8's automation depth.

Custom forms. ServiceM8's forms builder is more flexible. If you need bespoke site documentation beyond standard electrical certificates — risk assessments, client-specific checklists, asset condition records — ServiceM8's drag-and-drop form builder gives you more control.

Per-job pricing at low volumes. A sole trader doing up to 30 jobs a month can run on ServiceM8's free plan, and 50 jobs is £25/mo on Starter — both cheaper than Powered Now's £32/mo Professional plan. The economics change as job volume climbs.

Where Powered Now Wins

Compliance certificates as a native feature. On Professional (£32/mo per user) and above, every EICR, Minor Works Certificate, and Electrical Installation Certificate is produced, signed, and emailed from within the same workflow as the job and invoice. This isn't a workaround or a third-party integration — it's the core product. For electricians whose work regularly involves formal documentation, this removes an entire parallel admin process.

CIS reverse charge invoicing. Powered Now supports the domestic reverse charge — you choose a Reverse Charge tax rate on each invoice line. Its help centre doesn't say the HMRC wording is added automatically, so check a test invoice shows the wording your contractor expects. ServiceM8 supports the domestic reverse charge through a one-time setup — 0% reverse charge tax rates and a separate invoice template carrying the required note — but doesn't document CIS deductions. It's set up once, not edited per invoice.

Full offline working. Powered Now works without internet and syncs when signal returns. ServiceM8 works offline for jobs already synced to the phone — you can view and update jobs and save notes and photos — but final PDF quotes, invoices and Forms, and emails and texts, wait until you have signal again. For commercial sites with restricted WiFi access or rural properties with poor coverage, this is a meaningful practical difference.

Android parity. Every feature available on iOS is available on Android. No compromises for non-iPhone users.

The Catches

ServiceM8: Reviewers report the Android app lagging behind the iPhone one. Per-job pricing gets expensive at volume. No built-in compliance certificates. Reverse charge needs a one-time template setup; CIS deductions aren't documented. Pricing add-ons (phone system, SMS) add to the monthly cost.

Powered Now: Field engineers pay the same rate as office staff — no cheaper field-only licence. Test its Xero sync during the free trial. Quoting has more steps than Tradify's. Slightly longer initial setup due to the broader feature set.

Powered Now becomes the cheaper option for a sole trader doing high job volumes: its £28–£32/mo flat price doesn't rise with job count, while ServiceM8 moves to £59/mo above 50 jobs and £119/mo above 150. Who Should Choose Which

Choose ServiceM8 if

  • Your team is on iPhone, or you've tested the Android app on your engineers' phones during the trial
  • Client communication automation is a priority
  • High volume of short domestic jobs
  • You need powerful custom site documentation forms
  • Compliance certificates aren't part of your workflow

Choose Powered Now if

  • Any engineer uses Android
  • You produce EICRs or compliance certificates regularly
  • You work under CIS as a subcontractor
  • You regularly work in areas with poor signal
  • You want UK-native compliance built in from day one
