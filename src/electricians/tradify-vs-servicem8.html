Tradify vs ServiceM8 (2026): Which Is Better for UK Electricians?

Quick Verdict

Tradify for most UK electrical teams. ServiceM8 if your team is mainly on iPhone and client automation matters most. ServiceM8 says its Android app (ServiceM8 Lite) has almost all the same functionality as the iPhone app, but reviewers report it as weaker in practice, and one Capterra reviewer said their business had to change from Android to iPhone at the same time as adopting ServiceM8. If anyone on your team uses Android, test it on their phone during the free trial. Tradify works the same on both.

Beyond the Android question, they suit different businesses. Tradify charges per user per month, so your bill is the same in a quiet month as a busy one. ServiceM8 charges per job, which is cheap at low volumes and for teams, but climbs fast for a busy sole trader. ServiceM8's client automation and custom forms are genuinely better; Tradify's quoting is faster. Pick the one that fixes your actual bottleneck.

The Core Differences

Day to day, both cover the same ground — quotes, jobs, scheduling, timesheets, invoicing. The differences that should decide it for you are how you're charged, what phones your team carries, how much compliance paperwork you do, and how much you want the software talking to your customers for you.

Tradify is a New Zealand product with strong UK adoption. ServiceM8 is an Australian product. Neither is UK-native, and it shows in the compliance layer: neither handles CIS natively, ServiceM8 has no built-in electrical certificates, and Tradify only has them on its Pro and Plus plans. For compliance-heavy electrical work, that's a gap both share — and one reason Powered Now is worth a look.

The Android Problem in Detail

This deserves more space than a table cell. ServiceM8 says its Android app (ServiceM8 Lite) has "almost all the same functionality as the iOS app", including tracking time on site and clocking on and off. The gaps it lists are room scanning, drawing diagrams, editing PDFs and in-camera photo tagging.

Reviewers describe a bigger gap than that. A UK Trustpilot reviewer who moved from Apple to Android wrote that "The android app has barely any functions that the apple one does to the point where its pretty pointless" (Chris Harvey, Mar 2026), and another said "the android version needs a little bit of love" (Cameron, Aug 2026). On a team with mixed devices, test ServiceM8 on your engineers' Android phones during the free trial before committing.

Tradify doesn't have this problem. The Android and iOS apps are equivalent. If device standardisation across your team is not something you control, Tradify is the lower-risk choice.

One catch with ServiceM8's per-job model: a busy month costs more than a quiet month, which makes cashflow forecasting slightly harder. Tradify is the same every month regardless of how many jobs you run through it.

Where Each Tool Wins

Tradify wins on

  • Android. Full parity with iOS. No compromises for non-iPhone users.
  • Xero sync. Invoices, bills, contacts, and purchase orders all flow automatically.
  • Predictable cost. Flat per-user rate regardless of job volume.
  • Quoting speed. Building and sending a quote from a price list is faster in Tradify than in ServiceM8.
  • Simpler setup. Quick to get started.

ServiceM8 wins on

  • Client automation. Automated "on my way" texts, arrival windows, follow-up sequences — more sophisticated than Tradify's standard communications.
  • Custom forms. More flexible and powerful for businesses with specific site documentation requirements.
  • Low-volume cost. Per-account pricing means ServiceM8 is usually cheaper than per-user tools for teams — and free for a sole trader doing up to 30 jobs a month.
  • iOS experience. If everyone is on iPhone, the full app is polished and well-designed.

The Catches

Tradify: Every outbound email carries a "sent from Tradify" footer, which can look unprofessional on commercial jobs. Customer-facing photo reports only on Plus (£44/mo). No offline mode.

ServiceM8: Android users are on a lite app with missing features. Per-job pricing becomes expensive at high volumes. Australian product with basic UK compliance support.

Who Should Pick Which

Choose Tradify if

  • Any team member uses Android
  • You want predictable monthly costs
  • Your accountant uses Xero and expects clean sync
  • You're prioritising quoting speed and simplicity

Choose ServiceM8 if

  • Your team is on iPhone, or you've tested the Android app on your engineers' phones during the trial
  • You do high volumes of short, repeatable jobs
  • Client communication automation is a priority
  • You need powerful custom site documentation forms
