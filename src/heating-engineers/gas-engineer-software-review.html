Gas Engineer Software (GES) Review 2026: Is It Worth It for UK Heating Engineers?

If you need a CP12 app, GES is one of the first names you'll find. The real question is whether it should run the whole business, which plan you actually need, and when you'd be better off with something else.

Quick Verdict

GES is a strong fit if gas paperwork and annual servicing are the centre of your business. GES says it's built specifically for heating and plumbing businesses. A broad list of UK gas, oil and heat pump records is on every plan, the mobile apps work without signal, it reads your flue gas analyser over Bluetooth, and there's no contract. Independent ratings are high: 4.7 from around 1,500 ratings on the UK App Store, 4.5 from 453 reviews on Google Play.

The catches are about plans and price, not the software. The Starter plan (£19 a month, £18 billed annually) covers certificates and records: no quotes, no invoices, no scheduling and no service reminders. If you quote and invoice, you need Core at £25 per user (£23 billed annually). If you want invoices to flow into Xero, QuickBooks or Sage, which is how GES handles Making Tax Digital, that's Pro at £32. And the complaint that keeps coming back from long-standing users is renewal price rises. Trial it on the plan you'd really pay for, and check the price before you renew.

What GES Actually Is

Gas Engineer Software is made by Software Works For You Ltd, a UK company incorporated in 2012. It's a web portal for the office plus iPhone and Android apps for the van. GES says more than 7,000 UK businesses use it. Unlike the four general tools we review for heating engineers (Tradify, Powered Now, ServiceM8 and Jobber), it isn't a multi-trade product adapted for gas work. The workflow starts from the appliance and the certificate, then builds the quote, invoice and next year's service reminder around it.

It's under active development. Recent updates include online bookings from service reminders (November 2026), a data-badge scanner that reads make, model, serial and GC number from a photo (November 2026), an IGEM/UP/1B Edition 4 installation volume and tightness test calculator (October 2026), and air source heat pump service records (September 2026).

Certificates and On-Site Tools

GES lists these on its App Store listing, and its pricing page includes records and certificates on every plan, Starter included:

  • Gas: Homeowner Gas Safety Record, Landlord Gas Safety Record (CP12), Gas Warning Notice, Gas Service Record, Gas Breakdown Record, Installation & Commissioning Checklist, Non-Domestic Gas Safety Record, Leisure Industry Gas Safety Record, LPG Gas Safety Record, Non-Domestic Testing & Purging Record, Non-Domestic Catering Record
  • Oil: CD/10, CD/11, CD/12, CD/14 and TI/133D
  • Other: Air Source Heat Pump Service Record, Legionella Risk Assessment, Minor Electrical Works Record, Unvented Hot Water Cylinder

On every plan you also get Bluetooth readings from Kane, Anton and TPI flue gas analysers (Testo was added in a recent Android update), gas rate, pipe sizing and installation volume calculators, and more than 2,300 boiler manuals as PDFs in the app. As with any certificate software, check the output covers what the Gas Safety Regulations require before you rely on it. That responsibility stays with you.

Readings going straight from the analyser into the certificate came up in a Google Play review: "fantastic app, integrates with both my TPi and Kane analysers and TPi manometer, easy to use and also integrates with our Sage bookkeeping so makes life so much easier" — Paul Gannon, Google Play, Sep 2026.

How It Works on a Real Job

Take a landlord's annual gas safety check. The reminder went out automatically before the due date, by email or by text if you've switched SMS on. On Pro, the tenant or landlord can book a slot straight from that reminder, and it lands in your calendar as a job with an engineer assigned. On Core, they ring or reply and you book it in yourself.

On site, the engineer opens the job on their phone. The property, saved appliance details and job history are already there. If the boiler's data badge is new, a photo fills in the make, model and serial on Pro. They connect the analyser, the readings drop into the CP12, the certificate is signed on site, and it's sent to the landlord before the van's moved. The apps work without signal and sync later.

The invoice comes from the same job. The landlord can pay through an open-banking link or QR code on the invoice, and overdue invoices get chased automatically. On Pro, the invoice and payment then sync to Xero, QuickBooks or Sage. A reviewer running a three-engineer firm put the reminder side plainly: the software "has a very efficient reminder system for things like LGSR and boiler services which is great for generating repeat business" — Nico (GB), Trustpilot, Oct 2026.

The Catch

"From £18" doesn't include quoting, invoicing or scheduling. Starter is single-user and doesn't include quotes, invoices, scheduling or service reminders. If you're comparing GES with a job management app, compare it on Core at £25 per user, so you're comparing like with like.

Making Tax Digital goes through your accounting software, and that needs Pro. GES calls itself MTD-ready, but it doesn't send your quarterly updates to HMRC. It syncs invoices and payments to Xero, QuickBooks or Sage, which do the filing. GES's own help centre says the Xero integration "is available only on the Pro Plan". On Starter or Core, you'd still need to get your figures into MTD software another way. Our MTD guide is written for landlords, but the filing side works the same way.

Renewal price rises are the recurring complaint. Nearly every Trustpilot review is five stars, but the one-star review from November 2026 is about price. A sole trader and long-standing customer wrote that they were "outraged by the renewal hike from one hundred and twenty pounds to three hundred and seventy-five pounds a year" — Karl McVicker (GB), Trustpilot, Nov 2026. We can't match their figures to a current list price, so read it as one customer's renewal, not a published price. Before an annual renewal, check what you'll be charged and compare it with the current list prices above.

The extras are on Pro. Online bookings, appointment confirmations, accounting sync, the data-badge scanner and the AI features are Pro only. On Core and Pro, SMS reminders cost 10p a text plus VAT. None of this is hidden, but it's easy to trial everything for 14 days and then find the feature you liked needs the top plan.

A downtime complaint. One App Store reviewer complained in May 2026 of an evening lost to "yet more maintenance with zero notice" — App Store UK reviewer, May 2026. It's one review against a large, positive rating base, but worth knowing if you do your admin in the evenings.

Who GES Suits

  • Gas Safe sole traders and small teams whose income leans on servicing and landlord certificates. Reminders, appliance history and certificates are the core of the product, not an add-on.
  • Engineers who work in poor-signal properties. The apps are designed to work offline and sync when you're back online.
  • Oil and heat pump engineers. The CD/10, CD/11, CD/12 and CD/14 oil forms, TI/133D and air source heat pump service records sit alongside the gas records.
  • Anyone who wants to try before committing. No contract, a 14-day full trial, and self-service export of customers, jobs and invoices if you leave.

Who This Is NOT For

Engineers who only need certificates. Starter at £18–£19 a month covers certificates and records, but if that's all you need, a dedicated certificate app may suit you better (Checker.app is one; we haven't reviewed it).

Install-led businesses that live on quotes. GES does quotes and invoices, but its centre of gravity is servicing. If most of your work is boiler swaps and system upgrades priced from supplier lists, Tradify is built around that quoting workflow.

Multi-trade firms with electricians. GES's published certificate list includes a Minor Electrical Works Record but no EICR. If you also run electrical testing, a general tool or a dedicated electrical certificate app will fit better.

Sole traders on a tight budget who want automatic MTD sync. The accounting integration needs Pro at £32 a month. If you already keep your books in Xero or QuickBooks and don't need GES's certificates, that money may be better spent elsewhere.

Larger firms with a big office team. Commusoft says it's designed for businesses with six or more staff, which is a different kind of product with a different contract model.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Gas Engineer Software cost?

Three plans, priced per user per month excluding VAT: Starter £19 (£18 billed annually), Core £25 (£23 annually) and Pro £32 (£30 annually). Starter is single-user and covers certificates, appliance records, and customer and job records. Quotes, invoices, scheduling and service reminders start at Core. The Xero, QuickBooks and Sage integration is Pro only. SMS reminders cost 10p a text plus VAT on top. There's a 14-day free trial with every feature and no card.

Is Gas Engineer Software MTD compliant?

Not on its own. GES doesn't send your quarterly updates to HMRC. It syncs invoices and payments to Xero, QuickBooks or Sage, and that accounting software does the Making Tax Digital filing. The sync is only on the Pro plan (£32 per user per month), so on Starter or Core you'd still need to get your figures into MTD software another way.

Does the GES app work offline?

Yes, the mobile apps. GES says the apps are designed to work offline and online, so you can keep working without signal, and they sync with the web portal when you're next connected. You can also trigger a sync by hand. The web portal itself needs a connection.

Is there a contract with Gas Engineer Software?

No. GES says all plans are month to month with no contract and no setup fee, and you keep access until the end of the period you've paid for. You can export your customers, jobs and invoices from the web portal. The thing to watch is renewal price rather than lock-in: price rises are the recurring complaint from long-standing users.

What's the best alternative to Gas Engineer Software in the UK?

It depends what's pushing you away. If you only need certificates and GES feels expensive, a dedicated certificate app may suit you better. If you also fit a lot of boilers and quote heavily, Tradify is built around quoting. If service-contract work and offline CP12s are the core of your business, Powered Now is the closest general-purpose match. If you have six or more staff, Commusoft is aimed at that size of business.
