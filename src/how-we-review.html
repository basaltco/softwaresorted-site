How we review software

Our reviews are research-based. We work from vendors' own pricing pages and documentation, official guidance and published user reviews — never sponsored demos or vendor-supplied copy — and every page is built from sources you could check yourself. This page explains what those sources are, how we handle what we can't confirm, and how we keep pages up to date.

Where our facts come from

  • Pricing comes from each vendor's own pricing page. Where a vendor publishes GBP prices we use them; where it only publishes US dollars we say so, and any conversion states the exchange rate and the date we checked it.
  • Features come from the vendor's help centre and product documentation, not just its marketing pages. A feature that isn't on the website can still be documented in the help centre, so we check there before saying a tool can't do something.
  • Compliance rules — CIS, VAT reverse charge, Gas Safe record-keeping, Making Tax Digital — are checked against HMRC, HSE and other official guidance.

User quotes

When we quote a user, it's their exact words from a named platform — Trustpilot, Capterra, G2 or Software Advice — and every quote is recorded in a source register before it goes on a page. We don't paraphrase complaints into quotes, and we don't write "users say" or "reviewers consistently report" unless we can point to the reviews behind it.

When we can't confirm something

Sometimes a vendor's documentation doesn't settle a question — whether a feature is on a particular plan, say, or how a tool behaves offline. When that happens we say so plainly and tell you what to check during a free trial, rather than guessing in either direction.

What every review includes

  • A Quick Verdict up front, so you can decide whether to read on.
  • The catches — real limitations from documentation or user reviews, not balance invented to look even-handed.
  • A "Who this is NOT for" section, written with the same care as the recommendation.
  • For most trade reviews, the break-even maths: how much admin time the monthly cost needs to save to pay for itself at a typical labour rate.

Keeping pages up to date

Software prices and features change. Every review, comparison and guide is re-checked against primary sources at least every six months, and sooner when we learn of a change. When a fact turns out to be wrong, we correct it on every page where it appears, not just the one where it was spotted.

Affiliate links and independence

Some links are affiliate links, and the site may carry ads. The verdict on a tool is decided first, on its merits; an affiliate link is attached afterwards where one exists. Tools without an affiliate programme get the same treatment. More on this on our About page.

Spotted a mistake?

If a price has changed or something we've said is wrong, please tell us. We check every report against the source and fix what's wrong.
