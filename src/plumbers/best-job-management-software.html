Best Job Management Software for UK Plumbers (2026)

A plumber's week can take in a bathroom quote, a burst pipe at seven in the morning, a landlord's annual gas safety check and a day in a plant room with no signal. The right software depends on which of those fills most of your diary. This guide compares the four tools UK plumbers most often weigh up — Powered Now, Tradify, ServiceM8 and Jobber — on the things that matter in plumbing: gas and Legionella paperwork, how each one charges as your job count rises, and what still works underground. Pricing comes from each vendor's own pricing page, and user quotes are from named reviews on Capterra, G2 and Trustpilot.

Quick Verdict

Powered Now if gas safety records or Legionella assessments are a regular part of your work. It's the only one of the four with Legionella templates and full offline working, and it covers gas certificates from £32/mo. Tradify if most of your work is quoted domestic jobs — bathrooms, heating upgrades, refits — and paperwork isn't the bottleneck; it's quick to set up, works the same on Android and iPhone, and its Pro plan adds gas certificates. ServiceM8 for an iPhone team running lots of short call-outs, where per-job pricing and automatic customer texts pay off. Jobber for plumbing and heating businesses of three or more on maintenance contracts, where its customer portal saves phone calls.

All four have 14-day free trials with no card needed. Put a real week of jobs through your top two before deciding.

Why This Decision Matters

At £28–£44 per user a month on the per-user plans, this is a cost you'll carry every month, and the real expense is switching later: moving customer records and job history across, and getting everyone used to a new app in the middle of a busy season. It's worth choosing for the work you'll be doing in two years, not just this month.

The second reason is paperwork. Landlords need an annual gas safety check and a Gas Safety Record for every rental property. Landlords and building owners who manage Legionella risk need it documented. Notifiable work under the Water Supply (Water Fittings) Regulations 1999 needs a paper trail if the installation is ever inspected. If your software can't hold or produce those records, you end up running a second system — or a folder of paper — alongside it.

The UK Compliance Question

Gas Safe compliance. Under the Gas Safety (Installation and Use) Regulations 1998, landlords must have the gas appliances and flues in a rental property safety-checked every year by a Gas Safe registered engineer, keep the resulting Gas Safety Record (still widely called a CP12), and give tenants a copy. That's where the legal requirement for the record sits. For owner-occupied homes there's no legal requirement to issue a Gas Safety Record after a routine service, though many engineers leave a service record anyway. Tradify produces Gas Safety Records on its Pro and Plus plans (10 gas and 5 oil forms), and Powered Now includes Gas Safety Records and CP12 landlord certificates from its Professional plan. ServiceM8 and Jobber don't produce them natively.

Water Supply (Water Fittings) Regulations 1999. Certain plumbing works — installing water fittings, modifying hot water systems, connecting to the mains — are notifiable to the local water undertaker under the Water Supply (Water Fittings) Regulations 1999. While notification is the plumber's responsibility rather than a document the software produces, the paper trail matters if an installation is later inspected. Having job records, site photos, and spec documentation stored in one place is the baseline.

Legionella risk assessment. Under HSE's L8 Approved Code of Practice, commercial property owners and landlords are legally required to manage Legionella risk in hot and cold water systems, and plumbers are often the ones asked to assess it. Powered Now includes Legionella risk assessment templates as standard. The other three tools have no built-in equivalent, so assessments would go on a separate form or document system.

The Four Tools Worth Your Time

Gas, Legionella and offline

Powered Now — from £28/mo (£32/mo with certificates)

The only one of the four built in the UK for UK trades, and it shows in the paperwork. Gas Safety Records, CP12 landlord certificates, Legionella risk assessment templates and Water Regulations documentation are all built in rather than bolted on. That makes it the natural fit for a plumber with a book of landlord and letting-agent work. It also works fully offline, so a job finished in a basement or plant room can be completed on the spot and syncs when you're back in signal.

CIS deductions and Making Tax Digital are built in too, which suits plumbers who sub on new-build or refurbishment jobs. The certificates and Legionella forms need the Professional plan at £32/mo; the £28/mo Business plan doesn't include them. Even so, it's cheaper than Tradify Pro (£37/mo).

Catch: Everyone pays the same per-user rate — there's no cheaper licence for field-only engineers. Check the Xero sync with your accountant during the free trial.

Quoted domestic work

Tradify — from £34/mo per user

Tradify earns its keep on the quote. Price a bathroom or a boiler swap on site from a saved supplier price list, send it from the app, and turn it into a live job the moment the customer accepts, with nothing retyped. On the Plus plan (£44/mo), an AI assistant fills in materials from imported price lists, which helps when parts prices keep moving. It works the same on Android and iPhone, so it suits a team where the apprentice has a different phone from the boss. It syncs with Xero, Sage and QuickBooks.

Since a 2026 restructure, Tradify's Pro (£37/mo) and Plus (£44/mo) plans include gas certificates. The £34/mo Lite plan doesn't.

Catch: No Legionella templates on any plan. No offline mode, so it can't do anything in a basement with no signal. Every email it sends carries a "sent from Tradify" footer, which some commercial customers notice.

iPhone call-out teams

ServiceM8 — free for 1 user, then from £25/mo

ServiceM8 is built for days of short jobs. It charges by jobs completed rather than by user, so a team of call-out plumbers doesn't pay for every van. Its automatic customer texts — "on my way", arrival windows, job-done follow-ups — are arguably the most polished of the four, and they cut down the "where are you?" calls from customers waiting in for a leak or a blocked drain. On iPhone it's slick, and its custom forms let you build your own job sheets, though not compliant gas certificates.

Catch: ServiceM8 says its Android app (ServiceM8 Lite) has almost all the same functionality as the iPhone app, but users report it as weaker in practice. One Capterra reviewer said their business had to switch from Android to iPhone when it adopted ServiceM8, so test it on your team's Android phones during the trial. There are no built-in gas certificates (a paid third-party CP12 form is available from its Form Store), no Legionella templates and no documented CIS deductions. A solo plumber doing more than 50 jobs a month pays more than on Tradify.

Maintenance contracts, 3+ people

Jobber — from $29/mo

Jobber's customer portal lets customers approve quotes, check on a job and pay invoices without phoning the office. For a plumbing and heating business with a book of maintenance contracts, that's a lot of calls that never happen. Scheduling is built for several engineers, with route optimisation and live job status, and an offline mode was added in January 2026.

Catch: Priced in US dollars, with no GBP option. It jumps from $29–49/mo for one person to $99–139/mo as soon as you add a second. There are no gas certificates, no Legionella templates and no documented CIS support. Jobber documents a QuickBooks sync limitation: custom line items sync as a generic item. It makes most sense from three people, where its Connect plan works out cheaper than paying per user at current exchange rates.

Which Fits Your Kind of Plumbing Business?

By the work you do

  • Landlord and letting-agent work (gas checks, Legionella): Powered Now Professional (£32/mo). Tradify Pro (£37/mo) covers gas certificates but not Legionella.
  • Domestic installs — bathrooms, heating, refits: Tradify. Its quote-to-job flow is the strongest reason to pick it, and Pro adds gas certificates if you need them.
  • Reactive call-outs and drain clears: ServiceM8 for an iPhone team, where per-job pricing beats paying per van. A solo plumber on more than 50 jobs a month is cheaper on Tradify.
  • Subbing on new-build and refurbishment: Powered Now, which documents CIS deductions and the reverse charge (check which plan includes CIS).
  • Maintenance contracts, three or more engineers: Jobber, for the customer portal and the Connect plan's pricing from three people.

By team size

  • On your own: Powered Now Professional (£32/mo) if you need certificates. Tradify Lite (£34/mo) if you don't and Xero sync matters more. ServiceM8 is free under 30 jobs a month.
  • You plus an apprentice or second plumber: Tradify Lite if there's no gas work. Powered Now if gas or Legionella jobs are regular — £32/mo per user against £37/mo for Tradify Pro.
  • Three or more: Jobber's Connect plan works out cheaper than per-user tools at current exchange rates. ServiceM8 can also beat per-user pricing, depending on your job count.

What These Tools Don't Do

None of them is a substitute for WaterSafe or WIAPS membership — those are approved-contractor schemes, not job management tools. None of them run payroll. And none replace your accountant's own Xero or Sage setup for VAT returns and year-end accounts. They handle the admin between taking the job and getting paid.

If you grow past half a dozen engineers, all four can start to feel stretched. Commusoft says it's designed for businesses with 6 or more staff, and sells on 12-month contracts with quote-only pricing; SimPRO is also built for larger operations and contract work. Both take more setting up.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which job management software handles Gas Safe certificates?

Two of the four do. Powered Now includes built-in Gas Safety Records and CP12 landlord gas safety certificates from its Professional plan (£32/mo per user), with digital signature capture on site, and you can email certificates directly from the app. Tradify includes them on its Pro (£37/mo) and Plus (£44/mo) plans — 10 gas and 5 oil forms, including the Gas Safety Record — but not on the entry Lite plan. ServiceM8 and Jobber don't include Gas Safe certificates, so you'd need a separate system for them.

Does job management software help with Legionella risk assessments?

Of these four, only Powered Now includes Legionella risk assessment templates as standard. Tradify, ServiceM8 and Jobber have nothing built in. If you assess Legionella risk for landlords or commercial clients, Powered Now is the only one that keeps it in the same system as your jobs and invoices.

How much does job management software cost for a UK plumber?

On your own, expect £28–£37/mo: Powered Now Business is £28/mo without certificates, and £32/mo (Powered Now Professional) or £37/mo (Tradify Pro) if you need gas certificates. ServiceM8 is free for one person doing up to 30 jobs a month. A five-person team on Tradify Pro, the plan with gas certificates, comes to £185/mo. All four have 14-day free trials with no card needed.

Does job management software handle CIS reverse charge for plumbing subcontractors?

If you sub for main contractors, this matters. Powered Now's help centre documents built-in CIS deduction calculations and the domestic reverse charge, which you apply by choosing a Reverse Charge tax rate on each invoice line. Its website doesn't say which plan includes CIS, so check before signing up. Tradify has no native CIS or reverse charge option; you need Xero or Sage (QuickBooks isn't supported). ServiceM8 supports the reverse charge through a one-time setup (0% reverse charge tax rates and a separate invoice template carrying the required note) but doesn't document CIS deductions. Jobber documents neither.

Which plumbing software works without signal?

Powered Now works fully offline, so you can finish the job, the certificate and the invoice in a basement or plant room and it syncs later. Jobber added an offline mode in January 2026. ServiceM8 works offline for jobs already synced to the phone — you can view and update jobs and save notes and photos — but PDF quotes, invoices and Forms, and emails and texts, wait until you have signal. Tradify needs a connection.

Which tool integrates best with Xero for plumbers?

Tradify's Xero integration syncs invoices, bills, purchase orders, customers and contacts automatically. Jobber, Powered Now and ServiceM8 all integrate with Xero too. We haven't found reliable evidence to rank the four, so test the sync with your own accountant during the free trials.

Is there free job management software for plumbers?

Only for a very light workload. ServiceM8's free plan covers one person and up to 30 jobs a month, with no gas certificates. That can work for an installer doing a few big jobs a month, but not for a call-out business or a team. Everything else here is paid, with 14-day free trials. Invoice Ninja does basic invoicing for free, but has no job management, scheduling or compliance forms.
