Quick Verdict

ServiceM8 is the tool for a plumbing business that lives on domestic call-outs. It's clean and fast, and its automation is the reason to choose it — arguably worth the price on its own if you're doing high volumes of drain, leak and boiler-fault jobs: automated "on my way" texts reduce no-shows, arrival window notifications set expectations, and job completion follow-ups speed up payment. But there is one constraint to test before you commit: the Android app. ServiceM8 says its Android app (ServiceM8 Lite) has "almost all the same functionality as the iOS app", including tracking time on site and clocking on and off. The gaps it lists are room scanning, drawing diagrams, editing PDFs and in-camera photo tagging. Reviewers still report the Android app lagging well behind the iPhone one, and one Capterra reviewer said their business had to change from Android to iPhone at the same time as adopting ServiceM8.

If your team is on iPhone, ServiceM8 is a strong option for domestic plumbing call-out work. If you have Android users and the trial shows the Android app falls short for them, Tradify is the more practical choice. And if you do any gas work that requires Gas Safe compliance certificates, look at Powered Now (from its £32/mo Professional plan) or Tradify Pro (£37/mo) first — ServiceM8 has none built in.

What ServiceM8 Actually Does

ServiceM8 is an Australian-built field service management platform that covers the full job lifecycle: online booking, quoting, job assignment, scheduling, real-time field updates, invoicing, and payment collection. The automation layer is what sets it apart. ServiceM8 can automatically send clients an "on my way" text when an engineer leaves, a job completion summary when work finishes, and follow-up messages at configurable intervals. For plumbing businesses doing high volumes of domestic call-outs — drain blockages, leak repairs, boiler faults — that level of automated client communication cuts admin and is arguably more sophisticated than anything Tradify or Powered Now offer in this area.

The custom forms builder is the other headline feature. Build detailed site documentation forms — risk assessments, job-specific checklists, client sign-offs — from scratch using a drag-and-drop interface. For plumbing businesses with specific site condition requirements or client approval workflows, it's arguably more flexible than Tradify's basic forms. Assets management lets you track installed equipment at client sites — useful for maintenance contracts covering boilers, water softeners, and other installed plant.

The scheduling board is drag-and-drop: you can see where each engineer is, colour-code jobs by status, and move a call-out to whoever's nearest when a job overruns. Offline support is partial, which matters underground. In a basement or plant room an engineer can still open jobs already synced to the phone, update them and save notes and photos, but PDF quotes and invoices, Forms, and customer emails and texts wait until they're back in signal. Powered Now, by contrast, works fully offline.

You pay per account, not per person, so every paid plan lets you add as many plumbers, apprentices and office staff as you like. What you pay for is completed jobs. That suits a call-out team — a three-van business pays the same as a one-van business doing the same number of jobs — but it cuts the other way for a busy sole trader: at 200 jobs a month you're on the £119/mo Premium plan, where one Tradify Lite user is £34/mo. If your workload is seasonal, your bill will move with it.

Watch the extras. Customer texts come out of an SMS allowance — 100 a month on Starter — and each one after that is 5p, which adds up for a call-out business texting every customer. The ServiceM8 Phone system, which logs incoming calls as jobs, is an add-on from £15/mo. Storage is unlimited on every plan.

How It Works on a Real Job

A property management company calls about an emergency call-out — a burst pipe in a commercial unit. Here's how ServiceM8 handles it on iOS:

The call comes in through ServiceM8's phone integration, which logs it and creates the job. You drop it onto the nearest free engineer on the scheduling board, and their iPhone pings straight away. The customer gets a booking confirmation by text without anyone typing it, and when the engineer sets off, a second text says who's coming and roughly when: "Your plumber James is on his way, estimated arrival 2:15pm." On site, the engineer works from the job on their phone — details, time logged, photos of the damage, any custom forms. The customer signs off on screen, ServiceM8 raises the invoice with a card payment link, and the property manager can pay online straight away.

That workflow is where ServiceM8 earns its reputation. The gap for UK plumbers is the compliance layer: no built-in Gas Safe certificates or CP12 records, and no Legionella templates. Those still need a separate system or a paid third-party form. And if any engineer is on Android, reviewers report a weaker experience — test it before you rely on it.

The Catch

Android users report a weaker experience. ServiceM8's own line is that its Android app, ServiceM8 Lite, has "almost all the same functionality as the iOS app"; the gaps it lists are room scanning, drawing diagrams, editing PDFs and in-camera photo tagging. Users describe more than that. One Capterra reviewer said their business had to switch from Android to iPhone when it took on ServiceM8, and a UK Trustpilot reviewer who moved the other way wrote that "The android app has barely any functions that the apple one does to the point where its pretty pointless" (Chris Harvey, Mar 2026). If your apprentices or engineers carry Android phones, try ServiceM8 on those phones during the trial, not just yours.

No built-in Gas Safe certificates. ServiceM8 has no Gas Safety Record or CP12 template of its own. A CP12 form is available from its Form Store, but it's a paid, one-off purchase from a third-party developer. No Legionella risk assessment templates. Reverse charge needs a one-time template setup; CIS deductions aren't documented. For any Gas Safe-registered plumber, this is a material gap.

Busy solo plumbers pay more. Past 50 jobs a month a sole trader moves to £59/mo, and at 200 it's £119/mo — against £34/mo for one Tradify Lite user. If your job count swings between seasons, budget for your busiest month, not your average.

Recurring jobs have a scheduling quirk. One Software Advice reviewer notes that recurring jobs are calculated on 24-hour periods rather than working hours, causing them to display incorrectly in the calendar for jobs spanning multiple days. A minor but persistent issue for maintenance contract plumbers.

How It Compares

Against Tradify: it usually comes down to the phones your team carries and the shape of your work. ServiceM8's customer texts and custom forms are the more powerful; Tradify works the same on Android and charges a flat rate per person. An all-iPhone team doing call-outs will get more out of ServiceM8. A team with Android phones, or a solo plumber doing more than 50 jobs a month, is better off on Tradify.

Against Powered Now: No contest on UK compliance. Powered Now handles Gas Safe certificates, Legionella templates, CIS, MTD, and offline working natively. ServiceM8 wins on client automation and forms flexibility. For any plumber with compliance requirements, Powered Now addresses requirements that ServiceM8 simply doesn't support.

The Break-Even Maths

Take the Starter plan: £25/mo for up to 50 jobs. If you value your time at £50 an hour, it needs to save you about 30 minutes of admin a month — around seven minutes a week — to pay for itself. If you currently text customers by hand on every call-out and chase invoices yourself, the automatic "on my way" messages and payment follow-ups should cover that comfortably. At the far end, one Trustpilot reviewer reported saving 20–30 hours of admin a week; treat that as the ceiling, not the norm.

Who This Is NOT For

Teams with Android users who haven't tested it. Unless you're prepared to standardise on iPhone — and factor in the device cost — bear in mind reviewers report the Android app lagging behind the iPhone one, even though ServiceM8 says the gaps are limited to room scanning, diagrams, PDF editing and in-camera photo tagging. Test it on Android during the trial before committing.

Gas Safe registered plumbers needing CP12 or Gas Safety Records. These aren't built into ServiceM8 — only a paid third-party CP12 form from its Form Store. Powered Now (from £32/mo) and Tradify Pro (£37/mo) include them natively.

Subcontractors working under CIS. Reverse charge needs a one-time template setup; CIS deductions aren't documented. Powered Now documents built-in CIS and reverse charge support.

Plumbers whose workload swings a lot from month to month. Because you pay per completed job, a busy month costs more than a quiet one. If you'd rather know exactly what the software will cost, Tradify's flat per-user rate is easier to plan around.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does ServiceM8 work on Android for plumbers?

Yes — through ServiceM8 Lite, its Android app. ServiceM8 says it does almost everything the iPhone app does, apart from room scanning, diagrams, PDF editing and in-camera photo tagging, but users report a bigger gap in practice. If anyone in the business works from an Android phone, test it on that phone during the 14-day trial before you commit.

Can ServiceM8 produce Gas Safe certificates or CP12 records?

Not out of the box. ServiceM8 has no built-in Gas Safe certificates. A CP12 Gas Safety Certificate form is available from its Form Store, but it's a paid, one-off purchase from a third-party developer rather than part of ServiceM8. It has no Legionella risk assessment templates. For Gas Safe-registered plumbers, Powered Now (from its £32/mo Professional plan) and Tradify (Pro, £37/mo, and Plus) include gas certificates natively.

How does ServiceM8 pricing work for plumbing businesses?

By jobs completed, not by people. The free plan covers one user and 30 jobs a month; Starter is £25/mo for 50 jobs, Growing £59/mo for 150 and Premium £119/mo for 500, with no charge for extra users on paid plans. That makes it cheap for an installer doing a few big jobs and good value for a call-out team, but a busy sole trader soon pays more than with a per-user tool, and your bill rises in busy months.

Is ServiceM8 good for UK plumbers?

For an iPhone team doing lots of domestic call-outs without gas paperwork, yes — the automatic customer texts and custom forms are the reason to choose it. If you need Gas Safety Records or do CIS work, it's the wrong tool; look at Powered Now or Tradify Pro instead. And if anyone uses Android, test the Android app during the trial first.
