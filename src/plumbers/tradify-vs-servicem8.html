Tradify vs ServiceM8: Which Is Better for UK Plumbers? (2026)

Quick Verdict

For a plumber, this comes down to three questions, in this order: how much gas paperwork you do, how many jobs you get through in a month, and what phones your team carries. Tradify suits plumbers doing fewer, bigger jobs — bathrooms, heating upgrades, re-pipes — and anyone with Android phones on the team. ServiceM8 suits iPhone teams running a lot of short call-outs, where its customer texts and per-job pricing come into their own.

If you do gas work and need Gas Safety Records, settle that first. ServiceM8 has none built in, Tradify only includes them on Pro (£37/mo) and Plus (£44/mo), and Powered Now includes them from £32/mo along with Legionella templates. If certificates are a weekly job, read our Tradify vs Powered Now comparison before this one.

How the Type of Plumbing Work Changes the Price

The two tools charge in completely different ways. Tradify charges per user per month, whatever you do. ServiceM8 charges by how many jobs you complete, with users not counted. So the cheaper tool depends less on the size of your business than on what a typical month looks like.

The installer doing a handful of big jobs a month can run ServiceM8 for nothing, as long as they stay under 30 jobs. The solo plumber living on call-outs is the opposite case: once you pass 50 jobs a month ServiceM8 moves to £59, and one Tradify Lite user at £34 is cheaper. Add people and ServiceM8 pulls ahead again, because the apprentice and the extra vans don't cost anything on their own.

Two things the headline prices hide. ServiceM8's "on my way" texts come out of an SMS allowance (from 100 a month on its Starter plan), with extra messages at 5p each — worth costing if you text every customer on every call-out. And a busy winter month of burst pipes costs more on ServiceM8 than a quiet one, while Tradify's bill doesn't move.

Quoting a Bathroom vs Clearing a Drain

The two tools are built around different kinds of day. Tradify's strength is the quote: build it on site from a saved or imported supplier price list, send it from the app, and turn it into a live job the moment the customer says yes, without retyping anything. On the Plus plan its AI assistant fills in materials from those price lists — handy when you're pricing a bathroom or a boiler swap against parts costs that keep moving.

ServiceM8's strength is the customer who's waiting in for you. Its automatic texts — "on my way", arrival windows, job-done follow-ups — are arguably the more polished of the two, and on a day of back-to-back call-outs they save a lot of phone calls. Its custom forms are also more flexible if you want your own job sheets or site checklists, though they won't produce a compliant Gas Safety Record.

Basements, Plant Rooms and No Signal

Plenty of plumbing happens where phones don't work. Tradify needs a connection to do anything. ServiceM8 copes partly: jobs already synced to the phone can be viewed and updated, and notes and photos saved, but final PDF quotes and invoices, Forms, and emails and texts wait until you're back in signal. If you regularly finish jobs underground or in rural properties, that's a real difference — and Powered Now, which works fully offline, is worth a look too.

The Android Question

ServiceM8's Android app (ServiceM8 Lite) has, in ServiceM8's words, "almost all the same functionality as the iOS app". The gaps it lists are room scanning, drawing diagrams, editing PDFs and in-camera photo tagging. Users describe a wider gap: one UK Trustpilot reviewer who moved from Apple to Android said "The android app has barely any functions that the apple one does to the point where its pretty pointless" (Chris Harvey, Mar 2026), and another wrote "the android version needs a little bit of love" (Cameron, Aug 2026).

If you can't choose what phones your plumbers and apprentices carry, test ServiceM8 on their actual handsets during the trial. Tradify works the same on both, so the question doesn't arise.

The Catches

Tradify: Gas Safe certificates only on Pro (£37/mo) and Plus (£44/mo) — nothing on the entry Lite plan. No Legionella templates on any tier. No offline mode, which hurts in basements and plant rooms. Every email it sends carries a "sent from Tradify" footer.

ServiceM8: No built-in gas certificates — only a paid third-party CP12 form from its Form Store. Per-job pricing makes a solo call-out business dearer than Tradify past 50 jobs a month, and the bill moves with your workload. Customer texts beyond the allowance cost extra. Users report the Android app lagging behind the iPhone one.

Who Should Pick Which

Choose Tradify if

  • Your work is mostly quoted jobs — bathrooms, heating upgrades, refits
  • You're a sole trader doing more than 50 jobs a month
  • Anyone on the team uses Android
  • You want the same bill every month, and your accountant uses Xero

Choose ServiceM8 if

  • You're a small installer doing under 30 jobs a month (it's free)
  • You run a team on reactive call-outs, where per-job pricing beats paying per van
  • Keeping waiting customers updated by text matters to you
  • Your team is on iPhone, or you've tested the Android app during the trial

Doing regular gas work? Look at Powered Now first — Gas Safety Records and Legionella templates from £32/mo, and full offline working. Tradify needs its Pro plan (£37/mo) for gas certificates, and ServiceM8 has none built in.
