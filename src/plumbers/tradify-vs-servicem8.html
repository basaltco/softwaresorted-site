Tradify vs ServiceM8: Which Is Better for UK Plumbers? (2026)

Quick Verdict

Tradify for most UK plumbing teams. ServiceM8 if your team is mainly on iPhone and client automation matters most. ServiceM8 says its Android app has almost all the same functionality as the iPhone app, but reviewers report it lagging behind, and one Capterra reviewer said their business had to change from Android to iPhone at the same time as adopting ServiceM8. If anyone on your team uses Android, test it on their phone during the free trial. Tradify works the same on both.

There is also a third option worth naming: if you do any gas work and regularly need to produce Gas Safety Records or CP12 certificates, ServiceM8 has none built in, and Tradify only includes them on Pro (£37/mo) and Plus (£44/mo). Powered Now includes them from its £32/mo Professional plan, along with Legionella templates. That question should come first, before the Tradify vs ServiceM8 comparison even starts.

The Core Differences

Both tools cover the same ground: quotes, jobs, scheduling, timesheets, invoicing. Where they diverge is in pricing model, mobile platform support, compliance depth, and automation capability.

Tradify is a New Zealand product with strong UK adoption. ServiceM8 is an Australian product. Neither is UK-native. Tradify added gas certificates on its Pro and Plus plans in 2026; ServiceM8 has none built in, only a paid third-party CP12 form from its Form Store. Neither has Legionella templates. For compliance-heavy plumbing work, that's a gap both share, and it's why Powered Now exists as a separate option worth considering before this comparison matters.

The Android Problem in Detail

ServiceM8 says its Android app (ServiceM8 Lite) has "almost all the same functionality as the iOS app", including tracking time on site and clocking on and off. The gaps it lists are room scanning, drawing diagrams, editing PDFs and in-camera photo tagging.

Reviewers describe a bigger gap than that. A UK Trustpilot reviewer who moved from Apple to Android wrote that "The android app has barely any functions that the apple one does to the point where its pretty pointless" (Chris Harvey, Mar 2026), and another said "the android version needs a little bit of love" (Cameron, Aug 2026). On a team with mixed devices, test ServiceM8 on your engineers' Android phones during the free trial before committing.

Tradify doesn't have this problem. If device standardisation across your team is not something you control, Tradify is the lower-risk choice.

At current UK prices ServiceM8 is cheaper in every scenario above, because it doesn't charge per user. Tradify only comes out cheaper for a sole trader doing more than 50 jobs a month, where ServiceM8 costs £59/mo against £34/mo for one Tradify Lite user. One ServiceM8 catch: a busy month costs more than a quiet month, making cashflow forecasting harder. Tradify is the same every month regardless of jobs processed.

Where Each Tool Wins

Tradify wins on

  • Android. Full parity with iOS. No compromises for non-iPhone engineers.
  • Xero sync. Invoices, bills, purchase orders and contacts sync automatically.
  • Predictable cost. Flat per-user rate regardless of job volume.
  • Quoting speed. Building and sending a quote is faster in Tradify.
  • Simpler setup. Quick to get started.

ServiceM8 wins on

  • Client automation. "On my way" texts, arrival windows, follow-up sequences — more sophisticated than Tradify's communications.
  • Custom forms. More powerful for bespoke site documentation.
  • Low-volume cost. Usually cheaper than per-user tools for teams, and free for a sole trader doing up to 30 jobs a month.
  • iOS experience. If everyone is on iPhone, the full app is polished.

The Catches

Tradify: Gas Safe certificates only on Pro (£37/mo) and Plus (£44/mo) — nothing on the entry Lite plan. No Legionella templates on any tier. Every outbound email carries a "sent from Tradify" footer. No offline mode.

ServiceM8: Reviewers report the Android app lagging behind the iPhone one. Per-job pricing becomes expensive at high volumes. No built-in UK compliance certificates — only a paid third-party CP12 form from its Form Store.

Who Should Pick Which

Choose Tradify if

  • Any team member uses Android
  • You want predictable monthly costs
  • Your accountant uses Xero and expects clean sync
  • You're prioritising quoting speed and simplicity

Choose ServiceM8 if

  • Every single team member is on iPhone — guaranteed
  • You do high volumes of short domestic call-outs
  • Client communication automation is a priority
  • You need flexible custom site documentation forms

Important: If you do any gas work requiring Gas Safety Records or CP12 certificates, consider Powered Now first (certificates from £32/mo). Tradify only includes them on Pro (£37/mo) and Plus, and ServiceM8 has none built in.
