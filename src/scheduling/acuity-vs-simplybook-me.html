Acuity Scheduling vs SimplyBook.me: Customisation vs Payments (2026)

Of the four scheduling tools we cover, these two are the closest match for a service business selling more than a single simple appointment type — a therapist with packages, a salon with multiple treatments, a tutor charging per session. The decision between them comes down to where you want the complexity: in the appointment configuration, or in the payment handling.

Quick Verdict

Choose Acuity Scheduling if your priority is selling packages or memberships with detailed custom intake forms and varied appointment types. Choose SimplyBook.me if your priority is taking payment at the point of booking, priced in pounds. Acuity has no free plan (just a 7-day trial) and is priced in US dollars; SimplyBook.me has a free plan and publishes GBP prices.

What It Costs in Practice

Both charge per account rather than per person, but they cap different things. Acuity limits the number of calendars and lets you take unlimited appointments on every plan. SimplyBook.me limits bookings, staff ("providers") and custom features. Acuity bills in US dollars; SimplyBook.me in pounds.

At £1 ≈ $1.34, checked 6 October 2026, Acuity Starter's $16 is about £12, a little more than SimplyBook.me Basic's £10.90. For a small team, SimplyBook.me is usually cheaper — five providers on Basic against Acuity's six-calendar Standard plan — as long as you stay inside its booking limits. That's the thing to watch: SimplyBook.me's caps are on bookings, so a busy month can push you up a plan, while Acuity's plans don't limit appointments at all. If you sell packages and memberships, note that Acuity includes them on every plan; on SimplyBook.me, check which plan and feature slot covers what you need.

Setting Up the Same Service in Each

Take a physiotherapy clinic with three physios, offering an initial assessment and follow-up sessions, with a health questionnaire before the first visit. In Acuity, each physio gets a calendar, the assessment and follow-up are appointment types with their own lengths and buffers, and the questionnaire is an intake form attached to the assessment. Payment can be taken at booking through Stripe, Square or PayPal on any plan. With three physios you'd need Standard, since Starter has one calendar.

In SimplyBook.me, the physios are providers and the two appointments are services. Online payments, calendar sync and reminders each use a custom-feature slot, so check how many slots your set-up needs before choosing between Basic (three) and Standard (eight). Whichever you pick, the questionnaire will hold health information, so read the vendor's data processing terms first — see our GDPR guide.

The Catches, Side by Side

Acuity's catch: No free plan and a short 7-day trial, and configuring multiple appointment types and intake forms takes longer to set up than SimplyBook.me's payment-first model.

SimplyBook.me's catch: Feature-slot pricing — Basic includes three custom-feature slots and Standard eight. Calendar sync, payments and reminders fill Basic (£10.90/month billed annually); anything more means Standard (£20.80/month), and SMS messages cost extra as credits.

GDPR Note

Neither tool has a clear GDPR edge. Acuity's UK customers contract with Squarespace Ireland, and data may go to Squarespace's US servers under the Data Privacy Framework; its DPA is part of the Squarespace terms. SimplyBook.me is an EU company based in Cyprus, but its data centres are in Canada, France and Singapore, and its EU-storage commitment is made to EU-based businesses — so a UK business should check where its account's data sits. Frequently Asked Questions

Which is better for selling packages, Acuity or SimplyBook.me?

Acuity Scheduling — package and membership sales with detailed intake forms is one of its core strengths, more specialised than SimplyBook.me's equivalent feature.

Which is cheaper to start with?

SimplyBook.me, since it has a free plan (limited) and publishes GBP prices. Acuity has no free plan — only a 7-day trial — and is priced in US dollars.

Which is more GDPR-friendly?

Neither has a clear advantage. Acuity data may be transferred to the US under the Data Privacy Framework; SimplyBook.me is an EU company but holds data in Canada, France and Singapore. Read each DPA and check where your account's data will be held.

Which has built-in payment collection?

SimplyBook.me. Acuity handles package and membership payments, but in our view SimplyBook.me builds payment collection more directly into the booking flow itself.
