If you're a freelancer, therapist, coach, or consultant in the UK, you've probably looked at scheduling software and found the same problem — most guides are written for a US audience and bolt on a currency symbol. Few of them mention GDPR, where your clients' data ends up, or how you'd answer a client who asks.

This guide covers the four tools UK small businesses actually compare — Calendly, SimplyBook.me, Acuity Scheduling, and YouCanBook.me — based on UK pricing, the GDPR documentation each vendor publishes, and public review-site ratings. Doodle gets a mention for completeness, but it solves a different problem — finding a time for a group, not taking bookings — so it isn't reviewed alongside the four.

Quick Verdict

Calendly is arguably the best all-rounder for freelancers, consultants, and coaches who want fast setup and a generous free tier — but UK users should check the time zone handling before relying on it for client-facing bookings (see Catches). SimplyBook.me is the one to pick if you take payments and want invoicing built in — it's the only one of the four that handles payment inside the booking flow rather than through an add-on — though costs climb fast once you switch on extra features. Acuity Scheduling suits service businesses that want deep customisation of intake forms and packages, and are happy to spend more setup time getting there. YouCanBook.me is the one to know about if you'd rather use a UK-founded company — it has a free plan and bills in pounds if you choose, though it has been owned by a US company since 2025.

If you're a UK therapist, coach, or consultant and just want a recommendation: start with Calendly's free tier, and move to SimplyBook.me if you need to take payments at the point of booking.

Why This Decision Matters

At £0–£55/month, the subscription cost isn't the main risk — the main risk is picking a tool that creates a GDPR or client-trust problem six months in. Three of these tools hold data on US servers and the fourth uses data centres outside the UK. That isn't disqualifying on its own — each has a Data Processing Addendum and a legal route for international transfers — but it means you should read what you're agreeing to rather than assume it because the website looks professional. This matters more for therapists and coaches handling sensitive client information than for a freelance designer booking discovery calls.

The other real cost is easy to miss: feature-gated pricing. Several tools price their "starter" tier attractively, then each useful feature — calendar sync, online payments, SMS reminders — pushes you into a higher tier one feature at a time. Budget for the tier you'll actually need once notifications and payments are switched on, not the entry price.

GDPR and Data Residency

Calendly, Acuity (Squarespace) and YouCanBook.me hold data in the US. For all three, the Data Processing Addendum (DPA) is built into the terms you accept when you sign up, so there's nothing separate to sign, and each has a stated legal route for the transfer — the Data Privacy Framework for Calendly and Squarespace, Standard Contractual Clauses for YouCanBook.me. SimplyBook.me is an EU company based in Cyprus, but its data centres are in Canada, France and Singapore, and its promise of EU storage is made to EU-based businesses. None of the four is a clear GDPR winner: read the DPA, check where your account's data will sit, and think about what your booking form asks for.

This matters most if you're a therapist, coach, or any business collecting health, financial, or otherwise sensitive information at the point of booking — ICO guidance treats this as higher-risk processing. A dedicated breakdown of how each tool handles this is covered in our GDPR-compliant scheduling software guide — read that before committing if data protection is a live concern for your business.

The Four Tools Worth Your Time

Best for fast setup

Calendly — free tier worth using

Pricing: Free plan available; Standard from £8 per seat a month billed annually — Calendly bills new UK customers in GBP — or $10 a month billed monthly. Teams from $16 per seat a month.

Calendly's free tier is genuinely usable for a solo freelancer or consultant — one event type, calendar sync, and automated confirmation emails, with no time limit on the free plan. Setup takes minutes: connect your calendar, set availability, share a link. For anyone whose main need is "let people book a slot in my diary without back-and-forth emails," this is the lowest-friction starting point of the four.

Catch: Before the link goes in front of clients, book a test slot from a device set to a different time zone and check the confirmation shows the right UK time. We haven't found specific reports of a fault — it's simply five minutes well spent.

Best for payments + invoicing

SimplyBook.me — built-in payment collection

Approx. pricing: Free (£0), Basic (£10.90/month), Standard (£20.80/month), Premium (£45.80/month) billed annually; £12.90 / £25 / £55 billed monthly — GBP pricing from simplybook.me, checked October 2026

SimplyBook.me bundles online payment collection and invoicing into the booking flow itself, which Calendly and Acuity both require a third-party integration to achieve. For a business that wants to take a deposit or full payment at the point of booking — beauticians, tutors, consultants charging for first sessions — this removes a step that the other tools leave to Stripe or PayPal add-ons. Capterra UK rates it 4.6/5 across 1,291 reviews (checked October 2026).

Catch: SimplyBook.me prices by "custom feature" — each one you switch on (Google Calendar sync, online payments, SMS reminders) consumes a feature slot, and Basic includes three custom-feature slots and Standard eight. Calendar sync, payments and SMS reminders fill Basic; anything more means Standard (£20.80/month billed annually, £25 monthly). SMS messages are bought separately as credits on top.

Best for service businesses with complex bookings

Acuity Scheduling — deep customisation

Pricing: Starter $16, Standard $27, Premium $49 a month billed annually ($20 / $34 / $61 month to month), in US dollars only. No free plan; 7-day free trial.

Acuity's strength is customisable intake forms, package/membership sales, and the ability to set genuinely complex availability rules — different services with different durations, buffer times, and staff assignments. This suits service businesses with more than one offering (a therapist running individual and group sessions, a salon with multiple treatment types) better than Calendly's simpler single-event-type model.

Catch: The initial setup — particularly customising intake forms and package pricing — takes more work than Calendly's. Worth budgeting an actual setup session rather than expecting it to work in five minutes out of the box.

UK-founded, free plan

YouCanBook.me — UK-founded company

Pricing: Free plan (1 calendar, 1 booking page); paid Individual, Professional and Team plans, billable in GBP, USD or EUR, with 10% off yearly and 20% off two-yearly billing. Check youcanbook.me/pricing for current prices.

YouCanBook.me (YOUCANBOOKME LIMITED) is registered in England and Wales, founded in Bedford in 2010. For a UK buyer who'd rather their subscription money goes to a UK-domiciled company — a matter of principle for some, and occasionally of data-residency comfort — it's the only tool in the group with that profile, though it has been owned by US company Capacity since 2025.

Catch: Functionally it covers similar ground to Calendly without a meaningfully different feature set, so the UK-founded angle is the main reason to pick it — and that angle is weaker since the 2025 takeover by Capacity. Its data is hosted in the US on AWS, so UK founding doesn't mean UK data storage.

Where Doodle Fits

Doodle solves a different problem: finding a time that works across several people's calendars for a meeting, not letting a client book a slot in your availability. If you need to schedule a group call rather than take bookings from customers, Doodle is the right category of tool — but it isn't a substitute for Calendly, SimplyBook.me, Acuity, or YouCanBook.me, and we don't review it as a competitor to them here.

Need to take payment or a deposit at the point of booking → SimplyBook.me. Built-in payments and invoicing. Count the features you need: Basic has 3 slots, Standard (£20.80/mo) has 8. Who This Is NOT For

This guide is for UK small businesses booking appointments with individual clients — therapists, coaches, consultants, freelancers, tutors, salon owners. It is not for:

UK tradespeople — electricians, plumbers, heating engineers need job costing, quoting, invoicing, and compliance certificates (CP12, EICR) bundled together. See our job management software guides instead — a general scheduling tool won't cover that workflow.

Enterprise/multi-location businesses needing complex resource and staff scheduling across teams and sites — these four tools are built for solo or small-team use.

Anyone whose primary need is a CRM — none of these tools are a substitute for a proper CRM if managing customer relationships, not just bookings, is the core requirement.

What These Tools Don't Do

None of these tools handle invoicing beyond the booking itself (except SimplyBook.me's built-in payment collection), VAT/accounting, or contract management. They manage the booking layer — calendar availability, confirmation, reminders, and (for SimplyBook.me) payment at the point of booking. For businesses that also need quoting, job tracking, and compliance paperwork, a job management tool is a better fit than a scheduling tool — see our trades-focused guides for that comparison.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Calendly GDPR compliant for UK businesses?

Yes. Calendly holds data in the US and covers the transfer through the Data Privacy Framework, with Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK Addendum also in its DPA. That DPA is incorporated into its Terms of Use, so it's in place as soon as you sign up — there's nothing extra to sign. See our GDPR guide for the full breakdown across all four tools.

What's the cheapest scheduling software for a UK small business?

Calendly, SimplyBook.me and YouCanBook.me all have free plans. Calendly's free tier suits a single event type with no payment collection; SimplyBook.me's free tier is more limited, and its pricing page lists payments and deposits from the Basic plan up, not on Free — check before signing up if you need to take payment. Once you need calendar sync, reminders, and payments together, budget for a paid SimplyBook.me plan — Basic (£10.90/month billed annually) has three feature slots, Standard (£20.80/month) has eight.

Can I take payments through these scheduling tools?

SimplyBook.me has payment collection and invoicing built in natively. Calendly and Acuity Scheduling require a Stripe or PayPal integration to collect payment at the point of booking. YouCanBook.me's payment capability should be confirmed directly with the vendor before relying on it.

Which scheduling software is best for therapists or coaches in the UK?

Acuity Scheduling's package and membership features suit therapists running structured session packages. SimplyBook.me suits therapists who want to collect payment at the point of booking. Given the sensitivity of client health information, read the GDPR guide before choosing either — DPA and data residency matter more for this use case than for a general freelancer.

What's the difference between Calendly and Doodle?

Calendly lets clients book an open slot in your availability — it's a booking tool. Doodle finds a time that works across multiple people's calendars for a meeting — it's a poll/coordination tool. They solve different problems and aren't direct alternatives to each other.

Do I need a credit card to start a free trial?

No. Calendly, SimplyBook.me and YouCanBook.me all have free plans with no time limit, and YouCanBook.me's 14-day trial of its paid features needs no card. Acuity has no free plan but offers a 7-day trial with no card needed.
