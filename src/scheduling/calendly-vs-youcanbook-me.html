Calendly vs YouCanBook.me: Does the UK-Founded Option Win? (2026)

YouCanBook.me's main selling point for a UK audience is that it's a UK-founded company — but once you compare it feature-for-feature against Calendly, the practical differences are smaller than the "UK company" framing suggests.

Quick Verdict

Functionally, Calendly and YouCanBook.me solve the same problem in a similar way — calendar sync, booking pages, automated confirmations. Both have free plans and both will bill a UK customer in pounds. Calendly has the larger integration ecosystem. YouCanBook.me is UK-founded, though owned by US company Capacity since 2025 and hosted in the US. Unless the UK-founded angle genuinely matters to you, there's little to separate them — try both free plans with your own calendar.

Head-to-Head by Use Case

  • Want to try for free before committing → Either — both have free plans.

  • Want to pay in pounds → Either. Calendly bills new UK customers in GBP; YouCanBook.me lets you choose GBP.

When You Outgrow the Free Plan

Calendly's next step is Standard: £8 per seat a month billed annually for new UK customers, or $10 a month billed monthly. It adds unlimited event types, more connected calendars, automations and reminders, and Stripe or PayPal payments. Teams ($16 per seat a month billed monthly) adds round-robin booking across colleagues.

YouCanBook.me's paid plans are Individual (two calendars, two booking pages, custom colours and background images), Professional (six calendars, ten booking pages, automations, workflows, analytics and Zapier) and Team (ten calendars and fifteen or more booking pages per person, round-robin and role-based access). You can choose to be billed in pounds, dollars or euros, with 10% off for paying yearly and 20% off for two years. Its prices only appear in a browser, so check the current figures on its pricing page before comparing.

Setting Up a Discovery Call in Each

In Calendly, you connect your calendar, create a 30-minute "Discovery call" event type, set your available hours and a buffer between calls, and share the link — all on the free plan. In YouCanBook.me, you connect your calendar, build a booking page for the same call, set hours and buffer time, and either share the link or embed the booking form on your website. Both take minutes. The difference you'll notice is in how much you can change the look of the booking page, and on the free plans neither lets you remove its own branding.

The Catches, Side by Side

Calendly's catch: Check UK time zone display — test before relying on it for client-facing meetings.

YouCanBook.me's catch: No clearly differentiated feature set from Calendly, and the UK-company angle is weaker than it was: Capacity, a US company, has owned it since February 2025, and its data is hosted in the US on AWS.

GDPR Note

Both hold data in the US. Calendly covers the transfer through the Data Privacy Framework, with Standard Contractual Clauses also in its DPA; YouCanBook.me uses Standard Contractual Clauses plus the UK International Data Transfer Addendum. Both build their Data Processing Addendum into the agreement you accept, and both will provide a signed copy on request. See our GDPR guide for the full picture across all four tools.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is YouCanBook.me actually better for UK businesses than Calendly?

Not on features — the core booking workflow is comparable. The case for YouCanBook.me is about vendor preference (UK-founded) rather than capability, and that case is weaker now it's US-owned and US-hosted.

Which has a free plan, Calendly or YouCanBook.me?

Both do. Calendly's free plan gives you one event type and one calendar connection; YouCanBook.me's gives you one booking page and one calendar connection, with a "powered by" button.

Does either tool bill in GBP?

Yes, both can. Calendly bills new UK customers in GBP (Standard is £8 per seat a month billed annually), and YouCanBook.me lets you choose GBP, USD or EUR at checkout.

Is YouCanBook.me still a UK company after being acquired by Capacity?

It remains a UK company — YOUCANBOOKME LIMITED, registered in England — but Capacity, which bought it in February 2025, is US-based, and YouCanBook.me's own privacy policy says its infrastructure is hosted in the US on AWS.
